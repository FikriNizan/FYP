## 2. Temporal and label-maturity audit

**Recomputed from saved CSVs, independently of app tests:** a read-only standard-library audit traversed all 60,000 `monthly_flows.csv` rows and `targets_NOT_FEATURES.csv` rows. All 1,000 customers had the complete January 2020–December 2024 month grid; source-complete flags were true; inbound, successful outbound and combined amounts were nonnegative and reconciled in integer cents. The target was recomputed from a *positive trailing 12-month sum through cutoff* and the *next three months* using `8 × future three-month sum ≤ trailing 12-month sum`. Saved eligibility, binary labels and `label_observed_month = cutoff + 3 month-ends` matched all 60,000 keys: 46,000 eligible labels, 7,132 positives, 14,000 unavailable labels (11,000 warm-up and 3,000 at the end of available history). Equality belongs to the positive class. A score is possible without a mature label.

The April prepared file has exactly 1,000 unique rows for `2024-04-30`, 80 ordered columns (`cus_id`, `cutoff_month`, 78 predictors), and its original bytes match its manifest's length and SHA-256. All 1,000 April rows are feature-ready/scoring-eligible in `row_status.csv`. Their 1-, 3- and 12-month combined-flow means were checked against observed flow values **no later than April**; differences were under `0.000001` MYR, accommodating decimal serialization of float means. The 1- and 12-month fields also match the extracted April package. This directly checks the flow features, not every one of the 78 predictors.

The saved split assignments also passed a row-level maturity check: 22,000 initial training rows (December 2020–September 2022) have labels observed before validation starts January 2023; 3,000 internal-gap rows are excluded; 6,000 validation rows (January–June 2023) mature before October 2023 final test; 3,000 main-gap rows are excluded; 12,000 final-test rows cover October 2023–September 2024. All 46,000 assignment rows have the expected observation month. April **2024** predicts May–July 2024, so its label was not known on 30 April; its archived synthetic history allows retrospective label calculation only after 31 July 2024. No later observed months belong in the April dashboard snapshot.

**Source-time boundary review:** `feature_engineering_v1.py` assigns outbound submissions to submission month, but success/failure amounts and fault side to *payment* month; session starts to start month, completed duration/transactions to *end* month; login/friction to their event month; account data to snapshot month. It requires an accepted source-coverage ledger, checks complete customer-month keys, and uses an explicit predictor allowlist excluding target, future flow, IDs, names and latent controls. Its manifest reports 3,197 outbound outcomes, 2,936 outbound recency events and 9 session ends beyond the history boundary were excluded from corresponding aggregates. The producer also independently re-aggregates monetary flow/counts and reconciles the saved target audit. These are code/manifests reviewed here; the 9.4 million raw operational rows were **not** independently replayed in this note.

**Limit that must remain in the formal report:** synthetic availability assumes month-end snapshots are promptly available, outbound status/fault side is known by payment date, and completed sessions are known at clean end (including imputed ends). No `status_observed_at` or real ingestion-delay metadata exists, so this audit cannot establish point-in-time availability for a real bank. It verifies the frozen synthetic artifacts and the explicit producer rules; a real-source audit would require actual observation/ingestion timestamps and reconciliation before asserting absence of operational leakage. The application's CSV checksum and 1,000-row policy establish package consistency, not raw-source truth.

## 3. Latency baseline and proposed SMART targets

**Measured baseline, one local historical April replay (11 November 2026):** 1,000 rows, 795,001-byte prepared CSV, Windows 11 build 26300, Python 3.12.10 app environment, AMD64 Family 25 Model 80 CPU with 16 logical processors reported on this host; physical RAM was not captured for this web replay. Validation 0.126 s; trusted subprocess/model/native SHAP 5.594 s; parent evidence verification 0.039 s; publication before commit 0.109 s. These recorded stages sum to 5.867 s; the separate real browser submit-to-success observation was 5.995 s. Commit/network/render overhead is not isolated in the persisted publication stage. An unchanged-package browser resubmission took 0.217 s. The active worker has one scorer slot per Flask process; a busy attempt receives a retryable response. The configurable scoring timeout was 120 s. This is a **single local historical measurement**, neither p95 nor production throughput. Sources: `docs/MILESTONE_4.md`, ignored local `artifacts/monthly-comparison.json` and `artifacts/monthly-browser.json`.
