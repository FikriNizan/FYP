How to read the lines

The dotted line is the trailing twelve-month average of combined flow. The red dashed line is the attrition reference: half of that average. The defined drop is a next-three-month average at or below this reference. Those months are not yet observed, so the line shows the reference level, not an outcome.

Four words to know

Predicted risk

The model's estimated chance of a volume drop in the next three months.

Flagged

Risk is above the review line. A prompt to look, not a verdict.

Volume drop

Transaction flow falls to half or less of its usual level. The account may stay open.

Contribution at stake

What the relationship earns the bank over three months. An estimate, not a forecast loss. Called Revenue at Risk (RaR) in the project report.

Good to remember

Signals explain the score. They don't prove the cause.

Items marked AI are drafts for your review.

RM figures are estimates based on stated assumptions.

For data owners: reading Monitoring

Open Monitoring →

Outcomes check themselves. Uploading a month grades the assessment from three months earlier. While a month is missing, it waits; nothing is guessed.

The grid compares flags with real volume drops: Caught, False alarm, Missed, Correctly quiet.

Follow-ups count only when delivered (visit, contact or service fix) and dated inside the three-month window. No record does not prove no contact.

Input movement compares this month with last month. Descriptive only, with no alert threshold.

Rate-shock what-if is a stress test run offline on one saved assessment, named on the tab. It never changes saved predictions.
